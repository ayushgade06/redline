How a check works

An AI model is good at reading handwriting and bad at reliably judging algebra. So it only does the reading and the explaining. Whether a step is right is computed, the same way every time.

1
Reads your handwriting

A vision model turns each line into maths. Anything it isn’t sure about is highlighted for you to confirm or fix.

2
Checks every step

SymPy, a computer algebra system, tests whether each line follows from the one before. It also catches lost and extra solutions.

3
Hints, not answers

A nudge first, then the rule you missed. The corrected line only appears if you ask for it.

Built for self-study. Redline checks algebra and derivatives, one variable at a time. It doesn’t do geometry, word problems, proofs or integrals.
